# Code of Conduct

## Our expectations
We want Pick-One to be a friendly, respectful project for everyone on the team. We expect all contributors to:
- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Welcome different skill levels and help each other learn
- Credit others' work and disclose AI assistance where relevant

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or dismissing teammates' contributions
- Sharing others' private information without permission

## Raising a concern
If you experience or see behavior that breaks these expectations, contact any team member directly. If the concern involves a teammate, you may also contact the course instructor. The team will discuss it respectfully and respond within 3 business days.

## Enforcement
Team members may ask someone to change their behavior and, if needed, involve the instructor.
